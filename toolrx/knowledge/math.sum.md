# Math sum tool

Adds together a list of integer numbers and returns the total.
Use this when a user wants to add, sum, total, or combine several numbers
into one amount. Example: "add 5 and 7" returns 12.
Handy when you need an arithmetic total from a handful of small integers.

Longer requests and phrasings:
Calculate the total of these whole numbers and give me the combined amount.
Add up the following integers and tell me what they come to.
I have a few small amounts and I want their arithmetic total.
Work out three plus fourteen plus eight plus twenty and return the sum.
Combine these numbers into one value: five, seven, nine, and eleven.
What do twelve and thirty and forty-seven add up to altogether.
Sum this short list of integers for me and show only the final total.
Take these whole numbers and total them up.
